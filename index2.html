FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

A few quick answers about the services offered by P18.

1. What is included in Website Design & Development?

We create responsive, professional websites structured around your business goals, brand and customer journey. The exact pages, features and integrations can be planned according to your requirements.

2. What do SEO and AI Visibility services cover?

SEO can include a website audit, technical and on-page improvements, keyword targeting and ongoing optimisation. AI visibility focuses on making your business information easier to discover across AI-powered search and answer experiences.

3. Do you manage both Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Yes. Google Ads can target people actively searching for relevant products or services, while Meta Ads can help reach and engage audiences across Facebook and Instagram. Campaign scope and budget are discussed based on your goals.

4. What are Business Automations?

Business automations connect everyday tools and reduce repetitive manual tasks. Examples include lead capture, CRM updates, WhatsApp follow-ups, email notifications, task routing and AI-assisted workflows.
